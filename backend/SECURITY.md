# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@ombudsman.gov.example** (PGP key on the portal's About page).
We aim to acknowledge within 2 working days.

Please include a proof of concept and the request id from any error response.
Do not include real reporter data in your report - describe the path instead.

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith,
avoid privacy violations and service degradation, and give us reasonable time
to fix an issue before disclosing it.

## What we consider critical

Anything that links a reporter to a case. Specifically:

* any path by which a departmental account reaches contact data;
* any response, log line, metric, error message or timing signal that
  distinguishes reporters;
* recovery of EXIF, XMP or document metadata from stored evidence;
* forging a signed evidence URL, or reading evidence across departments;
* obtaining a case number from the intake store, or joining the two stores.

A finding in this class is critical regardless of how much access it needs -
"you would already have to be a DBA" is not a mitigation here, because the DBA
is in our threat model. See `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`.

## Out of scope

Missing headers with no exploit path, rate limits on public read endpoints,
self-XSS, and reports generated by scanners without a demonstrated impact.
